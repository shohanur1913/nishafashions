# Nisha Fashions Wear — B2B Apparel Backlink & Authority Building Strategy

A tailored, high-converting backlink acquisition playbook designed specifically for a Bangladesh apparel sourcing partner & RMG manufacturer targeting European, US, and Canadian fashion brands and buyers.

---

## 1. High-Authority Industry Associations & Government Portals (DA 70+)
These form the foundational trust baseline for Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) algorithm.

1. **BGMEA (Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers and Exporters Association)**
   - Profile listing in the official Member/Partner Directory under Knit, Woven, and Sourcing agents.
   - Anchor: `Nisha Fashions Wear - Bangladesh Apparel Supplier`
2. **BKMEA (Bangladesh Knitwear Manufacturers & Exporters Association)**
   - Listing under certified knitwear exporting units.
3. **EPB (Export Promotion Bureau Bangladesh - epb.gov.bd)**
   - Registered exporter database listing with valid ERC (Export Registration Certificate).
4. **Dhaka Chamber of Commerce & Industry (DCCI)**
   - Commercial trade directory profile with verified company address and contact details.

---

## 2. Global B2B Sourcing Directories & Marketplaces (DA 60–85)
Direct referral traffic from international apparel buyers seeking low-MOQ, high-capacity manufacturers.

1. **Fibre2Fashion** (Top textile portal)
   - Verified supplier profile with product gallery (Knit, Denim, Woven, Sweater).
   - Rich company profile link to `https://www.nishafashions.com`.
2. **Apparel Resources / Apparel Online**
   - Company listing in Bangladesh sourcing hub.
3. **Kompass Global B2B Directory**
   - Listing under *Textiles, Apparel & Clothing Sourcing Agent*.
4. **Europages**
   - Targeted European buyer directory translated into French, German, and Italian.
5. **ThomasNet & MFG.com**
   - North American supplier sourcing network for custom apparel cut-and-sew programmes.
6. **Global Sources & Alibaba Verified Pro Profile**
   - External verified manufacturer badges linking back to company technical pages.

---

## 3. Social Compliance & Sustainability Registry Links
1. **OEKO-TEX® Buying Guide & Directory**
   - Listing under certified supply chain facilities for Standard 100 textiles.
2. **Sedex Advance Platform**
   - SMETA audit reference link linking verified buyer audits.
3. **WRAP (Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production)**
   - Facility locator backlink.
4. **ZDHC Gateway (Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals)**
   - Sustainable wet processing & eco-laundry partner backlink.

---

## 4. Digital PR & Thought Leadership Link Building
1. **Textile Today Bangladesh (textiletoday.com.bd)**
   - Publish technical case studies on modern denim laser etching (Jeanologia) vs traditional potassium permanganate spraying.
   - Author bio link back to `https://www.nishafashions.com/categories/denim`.
2. **Just-Style / Sourcing Journal Commentary**
   - Contribute buyer quotes and market trends during global cotton price fluctuations or duty-free EU GSP+ updates.
3. **Medium & LinkedIn Articles by Merchandising Leadership**
   - Topics:
     - *"How European Fashion Brands Navigate 45-Day Lead Times in Bangladesh Knitwear"*
     - *"A Comprehensive Guide to Tech Pack Review and FOB Costing Breakdown"*
   - In-article contextual links to `/categories/knit` and `/services`.

---

## 5. Local Citations & Business Ecosystem
1. **Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business)**
   - Verified map pin at `92, Moneswar Road, Zigatola, Dhaka-1209`.
   - Links to `/contact` and `/categories`.

3. **Web Agency / Developer Credits**
   - Reciprocal tech partnership credits on portfolio showcases (`niloy.io`, `bangladeshhost.com`).
